NOVA CART / DISCOVER

What should NOVA CART build?

Use the case data to move from evidence to one focused digital intervention.

Recommendation: Reliability OS
CORE QUESTIONHow can NOVA CART improve the quality of growth?

The case shows user growth, but repeat purchase fell from 41% to 27%, cancellations rose from 6% to 11%, delivery time rose from 29 to 37 minutes, and support tickets rose from 3,100 to 5,900 per month.

PRODUCT THESISFix reliability before buying growth.

Build one operating layer that makes local inventory measurable, catches risky orders early, and gives customers a reliable promise.

Evidence chain

Connect the strongest signals in the challenge.

01
Inventory uncertainty

29% say products shown available can become unavailable after ordering. Some partner stores update stock only once every 1–3 days.

↓
02
Order failure

35% of cancellations are because products are unavailable; 23% of stores occasionally reject orders during busy periods.

↓
03
Customer friction

13% of orders are delivered more than 15 minutes late; support tickets reach 5,900/month, with refund status the largest category at 29%.

↓
04
Retention problem

Only 31% of first-order customers place a second order within 30 days, while customers reaching three orders have a 72% probability of ordering again the next month.
